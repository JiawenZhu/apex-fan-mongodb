# ApexFan 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:20 - Setup

Open the dashboard and state the premise: ApexFan is a World Cup 2026 group concierge for fans coordinating venues, nearby commerce, and shared trip budgets.

## 0:20-1:00 - Auto-tour and tool visibility

Click `Run Auto-Tour`. Point out the live tool chips:

- stadium collection lookup
- `2dsphere` retail proximity
- itinerary aggregation

## 1:00-1:40 - Proximity and surge

Change the venue selector from SoFi to MetLife, Azteca, or BC Place. Show that every host venue has nearby commerce data, surge level, capacity status, and VIP readiness.

## 1:40-2:15 - Group budget and CRUD

Use the shared budget prompt, then click `Add Activity via $push`. Explain that the itinerary updates the group plan and budget in one operation.

## 2:15-2:45 - Judge verification

Open `Judge Status Checks`. Show `/api/status` evidence and the playbook tabs for geospatial, aggregation, CRUD, and audit trail operations.

## 2:45-3:00 - Differentiation

Close by saying ApexFan is not just a single-fan trip planner. It is group coordination plus matchday commerce intelligence for real venue neighborhoods.
